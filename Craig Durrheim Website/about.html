Our Story

The Durrheim Luxury Lion factory will manufacture 2000 of the same lion drawings over 30 years.

All drawn by South African artist Craig Durrheim, a one man factory to maintain discipline and to create luxury.

All are available for sale except for a few special numbers, which will be available after the 30 years once the project is complete.
